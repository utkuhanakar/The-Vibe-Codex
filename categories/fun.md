# 🎢 Just for Fun

Because engineering shouldn't be all serious business. A collection of easter eggs, terminal magic, and cool scripts to flex on your screen.

## 🪄 Terminal Magic

- [CMatrix](https://github.com/abishekvashok/cmatrix) ![CLI](https://img.shields.io/badge/CLI-Effect-success?style=flat-square)
  - Simulates the falling characters from "The Matrix" in your terminal. Perfect for taking "hacker" screenshots.
- [Cowsay](https://github.com/piuccio/cowsay ) ![Classic](https://img.shields.io/badge/Classic-Humor-yellow?style=flat-square)
  - A configurable talking cow (and other animals) for your terminal output.
- [Neofetch](https://github.com/dylanaraps/neofetch) ![System Info](https://img.shields.io/badge/CLI-System_Info-blue?style=flat-square)
  - A command-line system information tool written in bash. Shows your OS logo in ASCII art.
